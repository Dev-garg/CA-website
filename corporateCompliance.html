Why Professional Compliance Matters

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) has significantly increased penalties for delays. Our goal is to ensure your "Zero Penalty" status.

Additional fees of ₹100 per day per form are applicable after the due date. For a typical company, this can exceed ₹50,000 in just a few months.

Failure to file returns for three consecutive years results in the disqualification of directors for 5 years across all companies.

The ROC has the power to strike off the name of the company if it fails to commence business within 180 days or defaults on annual filings.
